## Why

The Services section copy is in English while the target audience is Indonesian. This creates friction for corporate and individual clients who expect Bahasa Indonesia on a premium local service site.

## What Changes

- Translate and rewrite all text elements (label, heading, card titles, card descriptions) in `components/Services.tsx` into polite, natural Bahasa Indonesia.
- Apply `copywriting`, `stop-slop`, and `better-typography` principles: active voice, short sentences, no AI slop phrases, no em dashes, ≤25 words per paragraph, heading wrap guidance.
- Preserve all visual styling, layout, animation, assets, and font choices.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. No functional behavior changes.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a content/copy-only change.

## Impact

- `components/Services.tsx`: string values updated to Bahasa Indonesia.
- No new dependencies, APIs, or breaking changes.
